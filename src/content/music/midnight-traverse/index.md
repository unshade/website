Started as a four-bar loop in the FL Studio playlist between two work calls, ended up as the closing track of a set I never actually played. Built around a detuned supersaw pad, a sidechained sub, and a field recording of wind from a hike that got pitched down four octaves until it stopped sounding like wind at all.

- Arrangement: FL Studio 21, mixed entirely in-the-box
- Synths: 3xOsc, Sytrus, a couple of Vital patches
- The wind sample is real — recorded above 2,500m with a phone in a sandwich bag
