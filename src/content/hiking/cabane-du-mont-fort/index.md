Started from Le Châble, cable car up to Verbier, then on foot along the ridge to the hut. Clear morning, cloud inversion in the valley below by the time we hit the balcony trail. Grand Combin dominates the skyline the entire way — hard to keep a steady pace when you keep stopping to take photos.

- Route: Verbier → Chaux → Cabane du Mont Fort → back via La Chaux
- Best light: first two hours after sunrise, before the inversion burns off
- Bring a longer lens — the chamois keep their distance
