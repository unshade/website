Left the car park at 5:30 to beat the heat and the crowds — worked for the first, not really for the second. The last section is a marked via ferrata scramble, exposed but well-protected. Shot most of the descent on a gimbal, still cutting it down into something watchable.

- Early start is non-negotiable above 2,800m in July
- Via ferrata kit required for the final ridge
- Better views than the more famous routes nearby, far fewer people
