nstnr.com is the personal website of Noé Steiner. It has no accounts, no ads, no newsletter and nothing to buy. This page explains exactly what data the site handles, why, and what you can do about it.

**In short:** the site counts visits with a self-hosted, cookie-free statistics tool, so I can see how many people read it. That data never leaves my server and is never sold or shared. Content from other services (music players, Strava) only loads if you click to load it.

## Who is responsible

Noé Steiner, a private individual, is responsible for this website and the data it handles. Contact: [noesteiner@proton.me](mailto:noesteiner@proton.me).

## Where the site is hosted

The site and its statistics tool run on a personal server in my home, which I operate myself. No hosting company, CDN or cloud provider sits between you and the site.

## Visit statistics

I use [Umami](https://umami.is), an open-source analytics tool that I host myself on the same server. It tells me how many people visit and which pages they read, nothing more.

**What is recorded for each page view:** the page address and title, the referring website, your browser, operating system and device type, screen size, language, and your approximate location (country and region).

**What is not recorded:** Umami does not use cookies and does not store your IP address. It uses your IP address only at the moment of the visit, to work out your country and region and to create an anonymous visitor identifier, then discards it. That identifier is a one-way hash, built with a secret that changes regularly, so it cannot be traced back to you or used to follow you across other websites.

**Why:** to understand which content is read and how the site is found. This is my legitimate interest in running and improving the site (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). The statistics can't identify you, and you can opt out at any time (see below).

**How long:** statistics are kept with no time limit, since they don't identify anyone.

**Who sees it:** only me. The data is never sold, shared, or used for advertising or profiling.

**Opting out:** if your browser sends a "Do Not Track" signal, your visits are not counted. You can also stop your visits being counted with the button at the bottom of this page.

## Server logs

Like any web server, mine keeps technical access logs: IP address, date and time, requested page, and browser. They are used only to keep the server secure and fix problems, are never analysed for anything else, and are deleted once they are no longer needed for that.

## Content from other services

Some pages embed content hosted by other companies:

- **Music pages:** players from SoundCloud, Spotify, Apple Music and Deezer.
- **Hike pages:** the activity widget from Strava.

These are **not loaded by default**. Each one shows a placeholder with a button instead. Only if you click it does your browser connect to that service, which then receives your IP address and may set its own cookies, under its own privacy policy. Clicking "Always load" remembers your choice for that service in your browser. You can undo this with the button at the bottom of this page.

The privacy policies of these services: [SoundCloud](https://soundcloud.com/pages/privacy), [Spotify](https://www.spotify.com/legal/privacy-policy/), [Apple](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/), [Deezer](https://www.deezer.com/legal/personal-datas), [Strava](https://www.strava.com/legal/privacy).

**Hike maps** load their map images from [OpenTopoMap](https://opentopomap.org), which receives your IP address to send them, like any website you visit. It sets no cookies. Everything else (fonts, scripts, GPX files) is served from my own server.

## Data stored in your browser

The site stores a few small settings in your browser's local storage. They are never sent anywhere:

- your light or dark theme choice;
- your "Always load" choices for embedded content, if you made any;
- your statistics opt-out, if you used it.

## Emails

If you email me, I receive your address and your message. I use them only to reply, and don't add you to any list.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict its use. Because the statistics are anonymous, I can't link them to you. To exercise these rights or ask any question, email [noesteiner@proton.me](mailto:noesteiner@proton.me). You can also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in France, the [CNIL](https://www.cnil.fr)).
